## 11. What Broke Or Surprised Me

- The US regional profile capped again, but the global Sonnet 4.6 profile had a separate usable quota bucket and finished the corpus without needing a third region.
- N=10 mattered. Several tasks that looked clean at N=3 or N=6/7 dropped below the promotion bar after late failures, and some all-pass tasks still failed skeleton identity.
- The final headline is strong but narrower than the provisional sketch: 54 promotable candidates, not the provisional 57. Of the provisional set, 54/57 still held `>=9/10` DB reliability, but two of those failed skeleton identity and three dropped below the rate bar.
- There were zero infrastructure-error rows in the final checkpoint, so no uneven denominators remain.
